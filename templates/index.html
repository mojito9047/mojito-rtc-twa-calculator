Race Officer import

Imports the active course from the Race Officer app endpoint /api/current_race_course. It imports the full mark database, current course sequence, repeated marks, and port/starboard roundings.

Nothing is imported until the race officer has set a course. A shortened course is imported as it is being sailed, ending with a run to the finish (mark FIN). Waypoints show as Via legs.

Instruments

Where live wind, boat speed, heading and position come from. Directions are used as degrees true from every source. Choose Manual wind when no instruments are available; every display then shows that the wind is manual.

Race Officer

The Pwllheli Race Officer app the course, start time and postponements are read from. Importing and auto-import are on the Race Officer import page.

The club server, https://pro.pwllhelisailingclub.org, or a Race Officer PC on the boat network, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:5050.
